Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track a specific flight? ▼
You can track flights by using the Smart Search feature. Enter the flight number, airline name, or destination city in the search bar and click search. You'll see all matching flights with their current status.
What do the different status badges mean? ▼
On Time: Flight is operating as scheduled
Delayed: Flight is running behind schedule
Boarding: Passengers are currently boarding
Landed/Departed: Flight has arrived or left
Cancelled: Flight has been cancelled
How often is flight data updated? ▼
Flight information is updated in real-time every 2-3 minutes. You can also manually refresh the data using the "Refresh" button on any page.
Can I filter flights by status? ▼
Yes! On the Arrivals and Departures pages, you'll find a status filter dropdown. Select any status (On Time, Delayed, Cancelled, etc.) to view only those flights.
How do I create an account? ▼
Click on "Register" in the menu, fill in your details (name, email, password), accept the terms, and click "Create Account". You'll receive a confirmation and can then log in.
Is my personal information secure? ▼
Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your data. Your information is never shared with third parties without your consent.
